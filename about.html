About UR Fire Safety Solution

Headquartered in Vadodara, Gujarat, we are a single-point authority for life-safety compliance, equipment supply, and technical engineering maintenance.

OUR MISSION

"Making Every Building Fire-Safe."

UR Fire Safety Solution was established to solve a critical gap in fire protection: ensuring that every installed extinguisher, alarm sensor, and hydrant main is operationally fail-proof at the exact moment of a crisis.

We serve industrial manufacturing plants in Makarpura GIDC, commercial office towers, pharmaceutical labs, and residential housing societies across Gujarat. Our engineering team operates under strict adherence to National Building Code (NBC Section 4) and Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications.
