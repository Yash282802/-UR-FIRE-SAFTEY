Who We Are

UR Fire Safety Solution is a premier fire safety equipment supply and technical maintenance company headquartered in Vadodara, Gujarat. With a dedication to compliance, precision, and reliable protective systems, we serve as a single-point authority for safeguarding properties and saving lives.

From complex industrial spaces and expansive warehouses to local high-rise commercial structures and residential housing complexes, we design, deploy, and inspect robust fire prevention infrastructures. Our solutions strictly adhere to National Building Code (NBC) standards and Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications.

Our Mission

"Making every building fire-safe." We believe safety is not just a checkbox compliance requirement, but an absolute moral obligation. We dedicate ourselves to deploying fail-proof equipment so our clients operate with absolute peace of mind.
