1. Fire Extinguishers Supply & Consulting

We supply premium-grade, ISI-marked fire extinguishers specifically selected for different hazard levels. Correct placement and classification selection is critical to ensuring rapid suppression when a hazard occurs.

Classifications Supplied:

• ABC Dry Powder: Multi-purpose protection suitable for wood, paper, liquids, and electrical fires.

• Carbon Dioxide (CO2): Premium gas-based discharge ideal for delicate server rooms and heavy electrical switchgear.

• Foam & Water: Tailored for combustible solid materials and fuel/oil fires.

2. Fire Alarm & Detection Systems

Early warning is the difference between a minor incident and a complete disaster. Our engineers design and install high-sensitivity smoke, heat, and flame detection system networks tailored to the physical characteristics of your building layout.

Systems Configured:

• Addressable Alarm Networks: Precise location mapping for massive industrial complexes and commercial centers.

• Conventional Systems: Cost-effective zone-based detection setups for smaller offices and residential housing.

• Integrated Alarm Testing: Periodic maintenance and calibration of heat/smoke sensors and manual call points.

3. High-Pressure Hydrant Networks

We undertake complete turnkey design, piping supply, installation, and commissioning of outdoor and indoor fire hydrant networks. All installations are executed under strict compliance guidelines from national and regional safety standards.

Technical Scope:

• Infrastructure: High-pressure water reservoirs, diesel-powered pump stations, ring main piping networks.

• Terminals: Wet riser installations, external double-outlet hydrants, indoor hose cabinets, and canvas fire hoses.

• Engineering Compliance: Full alignment with NBC Section 4 standards and IS 13039 safety guidelines.

4. Hydrotesting, Refilling & AMC Contracts

Fire safety hardware is only as good as its maintenance cycle. Over time, pressure loss, powder compaction, and seals leakage can render an extinguisher useless. We provide robust Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) to keep you fully protected.

Service Elements:

• On-Site Verification: Weight inspections, pressure gauge calibration, and structural checks.

• Hydrostatic Testing: High-pressure water expansion testing of cylinders to identify microscopic shell cracks.

• Certified Refilling: Refilling of dry chemical powder (MAP 90%), CO2 gas, or foam agents with official safety tags.

5. Certified Personal Protective Equipment

We supply industrial sites, electrical panel operators, and building emergency teams with fully certified protective apparel designed to resist radiant heat, mechanical impacts, and chemical hazards.

Product Suite:

• Apparel: Nomex/Kevlar multi-layer fire-resistant suits, industrial gloves, and heavy-duty safety boots.
